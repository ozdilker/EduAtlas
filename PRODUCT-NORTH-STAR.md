## Canonical source

The product north star for EduAtlas is defined in **`PRD.md`**.

### Mission

> EduAtlas helps parents discover, compare, and contact educational institutions across Türkiye — starting with private schools, dershaneler, etüt merkezleri, language schools, kindergartens, and preschool institutions.

### Design implication

All Design Kit assets and visual work must serve this mission: **reduce family decision anxiety**, enable trustworthy discovery, and support institution contact — never marketplace hype or invented UI fashion.
